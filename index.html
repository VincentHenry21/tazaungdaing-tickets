How redemption works

One ticket, one ID, one scan. The QR and the printed ID always match.

  1. Buy and get issued

    The buyer name and phone go on the ticket, a unique Ticket ID is generated, and a QR code is created from that ID, name and phone. Only 1 ticket is issued for each name and phone number, and no two tickets share an ID.

  2. Show the ticket at the counter

    Issued tickets are saved on this device until the event, so closing the tab does not lose them. Tap Save QR as PNG to also keep the QR image in Photos or Files. Reopen this page on the same device, flip the ticket to the QR side, and let the counter scan it. The buyer can also read out the Ticket ID printed on both sides.

  3. Counter scans and redeems

    The counter checks the scan in the panel on this page. A valid ticket is marked redeemed and stamped, and a second scan of the same ticket is rejected, so one ticket admits one person, once.

Event counter · Check and redeem

For counter staff. Scan the buyer QR with the camera, or type the Ticket ID printed on the ticket, then redeem it. Tickets issued on this device are saved on this device until the event, so they can still be checked and redeemed here after this tab has been closed and reopened.
